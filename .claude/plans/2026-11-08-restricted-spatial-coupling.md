# Restricted spatial coupling feasibility

This is a new bounded investigation; the rejected whole-state reconstruction and old checkpoint tickets remain closed. One implementation writer, independent review, no production or shared Geant4 changes, no full-dose run, PR or push.

## Proposed method and first gate

Keep the native shared particle process for the union of active reaction neighborhoods and pre-reserved future source neighborhoods. Overlaps merge geometrically without evolving independent tracks. Never promote an already coarse cell by inventing positions: reject a schedule whose future source reaches such a cell. Transfer needs resolved precursors, no pending source in its reserved space-time region, and a validated bound on omitted encounters and descendants. Cell size or elapsed age alone is insufficient. A common depleted bulk reservoir and absolute source/observation ordering are mandatory.

The smallest experiment first measures whether spatial eligibility can justify a transfer. Run native shared histories and matched old/fresh singles; capture live positions at endpoints without restart. Offline endpoint diagnostics report the fraction having another particle within 20/40/80 nm plus the cell half-diagonal for 20/25/50 nm cells. These are deliberately conservative geometric diagnostics, not chemical error bounds. Also report unresolved precursors, shared-source ancestry, exact source registrations, energy/mass and bulk oxygen. No molecule is actually transferred before a chemical admissibility condition is justified.

## Fixed protocol before comparisons

Regime: existing independent physical packet pairs in a 4 um cube, PureWater, native IRT_syn source-contact kernel, matched reflecting boundary, two primary arrivals at 0 and 1.5 ps; precursor activation at 1 and 2.5 ps. Oxygen-free and 21% O2 controls. Endpoints 50, 100 and 150 ps are separate complete histories, not scheduler interruptions. Eight physical pairs per oxygen condition; two chemistry replicas each; shared, old-only and fresh-only arms: 288 histories. Physical pairs are the sampling units, never chemistry replicas. Existing libraries are reused as fixed independent physical clusters, not resampled into an independent beam. Oxygen libraries are independently generated; oxygen contrasts are unpaired across libraries.

Observables: OH, e_aq, H2O2, HO2, total survivors, disjoint and mixed ancestry reactions, oxygen pool changes, unresolved precursor count, geometric retention fraction at each radius/cell size, source count/registrations/energy/mass and elapsed/peak private memory. Shared minus matched singles measures nonadditivity; it is not a proposed merged-track method. Average replicas within physical pair; descriptive t(7) 95% intervals separate between-cluster variability from within-cluster replica spread. No optional sampling expansion. Sparse two-replica variance is imprecise.

Margins: exact zero discrepancy for source/timing/energy/population registration and handover budgets. Geometric classification must be monotone with guard radius/cell diagonal. Chemical equivalence requires a scientifically justified downstream error budget; the previous exploratory max(5%,1 molecule) is not such a budget. None is supplied by the existing evidence. Consequently this gate cannot issue chemical GO on small sample differences. The only justified zero-error transfer condition currently available is no transfer (native fallback), which is not coupling validation. A failure to certify a nonzero transfer yields NO-GO for this proposed accelerated regime, not proof that all hybrid methods are impossible.

Caps: serial complete-history jobs, 256 MiB committed-private-memory per process, 10 s per history, 3 GiB system-free-memory floor, 288 histories maximum; stop on cap rather than relaxing it. Build separately with four compile jobs, no kernel rebuild. Preserve new raw outputs and before/after executable/runtime/packet hashes. No overwrite of old evidence. Native source audit and existing UHDR patterns precede code. Independent reviewer reads this protocol before runs. Validated experiment/report changes are committed by parent after review.

Population audit means actual final BasicCounter counts versus live eligible species, as in the accepted reference, plus exact activation registration and the source energy ledger. It is not constancy of total molecular count across reactions. The snapshot includes all live configurations; unresolved water precursors are counted separately. Record minimum pair separation (infinity for fewer than two survivors). Geometric retained fractions use all live survivors as denominator; empty states have count zero and fraction reported unavailable. Arbitrary 20/40/80 nm radii are sensitivity probes, not first-passage certificates. No sources arrive after 2.5 ps in this gate; future-region occupancy after these endpoints is therefore empty by construction, not an arbitrary-beam result. Actual chemical spatial/handover convergence remains untested. No inference of absence of cross-source effects follows from zero observed cross reactions in distinct untransformed pairs.

Before any comparisons, add nine capped mechanism controls: three seeds each for near-contact particle retention, separated same-cell retention, and engineered bulk depletion continuity. These use the existing native controls; the old reconstruction arm is not rerun or reused as a candidate. Maximum 297 complete jobs. The contact electron yield distinction has a strict zero-error criterion because the initialized contact probability is one; its matching cell count proves coarse counts are not sufficient state for a fresh arrival. This is a mechanistic falsification, not an end-use yield tolerance. The bulk control removes seven molecules through the public bulk API and checks persistence over source stages; it is not physical depletion from dilute packets.
